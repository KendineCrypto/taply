# Taply — Brand Kit v2.0 (Corrected Geometry)

**Approved direction:** `taply.` wordmark and `t.` app icon in deep forest green, warm cream, and bright lime.

## Geometry: single master mark

The `t` glyph is not separately typeset. The **identical literal SVG path** is inserted into every icon AND wordmark. The dot coordinates/radius in all icon variants are also identical. Dark and light colorways only invert cream and night fills. All SVG assets are path-based; the `aply` lettering has been converted from Inter Display Bold to vector outlines so the logo does **not** depend on locally installed fonts.

For clarity, this is a **carefully reconstructed vector master based on the approved appearance**; it is not an automated pixel-perfect trace of the raster reference. Visual approval and possible optical refinements should precede trademarks or mass printing.

## Brand colors

- Night `#0B120E` — primary background
- Forest `#111C15` — cards and surfaces
- Olive `#203226` — elevated backgrounds
- Cream `#F4F3E8` — main text and mark
- Lime `#C7FC75` — dot and key CTAs

## Typographic guidance

**Logo:** use provided SVG paths only; do not recreate as live font text or add extra spaces before dot. **UI:** Inter / Inter Display, with sensible system fallback. Do not redistribute font files with the kit.

## Usage rules

Do not shift the dot away from the letter; do not substitute a generic font's `t` into the mark. Don't stretch, rotate or add heavy glow to production logo files. Minimum clearspace: at least the dot diameter on every side. Use the same SVG path for app icons, web navbar, and merchant checkout receipts.

## NEAR relationship

Taply brand colors are Taply's own; do not call lime/olive the official NEAR branding. Mark the app as `NEAR Testnet` separately.
